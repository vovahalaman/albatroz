# Regional cues

## Language and text
- PT: ã õ ç, "-ção", "Rua", "Farmácia", "Pastelaria", "Talho".
- ES: ñ, "Calle", "Avenida", "Farmacia", "Panadería".
- FR: é è ê à ç, "Rue", "Boulangerie", "Pharmacie", "Tabac".
- Co-official languages: Galician ("Rúa", close to PT!), Catalan ("Carrer", "Plaça"), Basque ("kalea", tx/tz).

## Licence plates (format only)
- PT: EU band on the left; older plates have a yellow date band on the right.
- ES: EU band on the left only, `0000 XXX`.
- FR: blue bands on both sides; département number on the right.

## Brands and street furniture
- PT: red CTT mailboxes, "Multibanco" ATMs.
- ES: yellow Correos mailboxes.
- FR: yellow La Poste mailboxes.

## Pavement and architecture
- PT: calçada portuguesa (patterned stone paving), azulejo façades.
- Alentejo/Algarve: whitewash with coloured trim (blue/yellow).
- Algarve: decorative chimneys.
- North PT: granite. Central PT: schist villages.

## Vegetation and land use
- Eucalyptus plantations (central/north PT).
- Cork and holm oak *montado/dehesa* (Alentejo, Extremadura).
- Terraced vineyards (Douro).
- Summer-dry yellow grass (season).
- Laurel forest (Madeira, Canaries), volcanic terrain (Azores, Canaries).

## Checklist dimensions
vegetation · relief · soil colour · water/coast (+yaw) · roof type/colour · wall finish · chimneys · pavement · road markings · bollards/poles · signage shape/colour · vehicles · season.
